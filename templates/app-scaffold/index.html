你的应用，从这句话开始

Your app starts with one sentence

在 MTNode「应用中心 → 开发」的会话栏里写下你想要什么 —— 这个页面就会变成你要的样子。

Write what you want in the chat box under App Center → Develop, and this page becomes it.

  1. 说一句就行：比如「做一个便签墙，能加、能删、能保存」。
  2. One sentence is enough: for example, “a sticky-note wall I can add to, delete from and keep”.
  3. 马上看到变化：说完这一句，这个页面会自己刷新成新的样子。
  4. Watch it change: once you send that sentence, this page reloads itself into the new version.
  5. 不满意就继续说：「标题大一点」「换成浅色」「加个搜索框」，一轮一轮改到满意。
  6. Keep talking: “a bigger title”, “a light theme”, “add a search box” — round by round, until it fits.

不用写代码，也不用看懂任何技术名词。下面这块便签是示例，替换掉它就是你的应用。

No code, and no technical vocabulary needed. The note demo below is sample content — replace it and the app is yours.
