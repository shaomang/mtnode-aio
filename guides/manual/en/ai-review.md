# AI review: notes & revisions

> In one sentence: revise and review text that already exists — by hand, or by letting the AI edit it again from whole-document notes and local notes.

![Output box → Review → Notes → Let AI revise](img/mtnode-edit-01-ui.svg)

*Figure: click "Review" in the output box; whole-document notes and local notes are both written here, then the AI revises from those notes.*

## Text editing (review) · Node description

### What text editing (review) is

Text from a single generation rarely satisfies you completely. Review lets you, after the AI has generated a text, edit that existing text again by hand, with AI whole-document editing and with AI local editing: it is used for proofreading, polishing, expanding, grammar checking and so on.

### How to use it

Once a text has been generated, click the "Review" button in the output box on the right to open the review interface. A whole-document note carries an overall revision request; for a local note, first click the "Local note" button at the top to turn that feature on, then select the content you want changed locally.
