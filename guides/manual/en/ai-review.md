# AI review & text editing

Long drafts rarely come out perfect on the first try. Instead of rewriting everything yourself or starting a new prompt from scratch, use **AI review** to highlight exact lines, leave notes, and let AI revise only what needs work.

![AI review loop](img/mtnode-edit-01-ui.svg)

## Revising drafts with AI review

1. **Open Review**: After a text node finishes, click **Review** in the output box header.
2. **Leave notes**:
   - **Inline note**: Highlight a sentence or paragraph and write what to change (e.g., "Make this friendlier" or "Add a concrete example").
   - **Document note**: Add an overall note for tone, structure, or word count.
3. **Let AI revise**: Click to revise from your notes. AI produces a fresh version addressing each note.
4. **Compare, roll back, or adopt**:
   - Every revision is kept in full so you can compare versions anytime.
   - Click **Roll back** to restore an earlier version as the active draft.
   - Click **Adopt current version** when you're happy to write it back to the node.

> **Notes & screenshots in sessions too**: In Agent Sessions, you can also highlight text to leave review notes — and click **Insert image** on a note card to attach screenshots right alongside your feedback.

## Built-in Markdown editor

Text nodes, review windows, project file previews, and research notes all share the same clean Markdown editor:

- **Switch views freely**: Edit cleanly in **WYSIWYG / Reading** mode (headings, tables, and math rendered live), or switch to **Source** mode (split editor + live preview) for complex tables and code blocks.
- **Indent effortlessly**: Press `Tab` to indent and `Shift + Tab` to outdent single or multiple selected lines.
- **Clean paste**: Copy from web pages, Word, or chat apps and press `Ctrl + V` directly.
- **Hands-off safety**: Opening a file just to read it never reformats or overwrites your text unless you make an edit.
