# Build a desktop app

Want a handy little desktop app — a budget tracker, prompt card box, countdown board, or a clean front-end for one of your canvas workflows? In MTNode's **Apps** hub, you don't need build tools or web frameworks: pick a look, chat with AI, preview live, and launch it in its own window.

![Building a desktop app through conversation](img/mtnode-app-02-ui.svg)

## Three tabs in Apps

Open **Apps** from the top bar:

- **Apps**: Browse community-published apps, download and launch them, or switch between versions and author forks.
- **Library**: Manage apps you downloaded or built yourself, and launch any of them in a standalone window.
- **Develop**: Your app workshop — pick apps and dev sessions on the left, see a live preview in the center, and iterate with AI on the right.

## Build your first app in 4 steps

1. **Click "New app" at the top left**: Name your app and pick from **7 visual styles** (Minimal, Tech, Warm, Editorial, Terminal, Glass, Retro — spanning dark and light looks, each with a real preview) or pick **Custom** so AI asks what vibe you want first.
2. **Describe what you want in chat**: For example, "Turn this into a daily task board with groups on the left, priority filters on top, and automatic saving."
3. **Preview & test live**: The center column updates as AI edits. Its header gives you quick buttons: **Open folder 📂 · Launch window · Publish · Refresh · App settings · Canvas**.
4. **Iterate freely**: Ask for layout tweaks, new features, or a style swap anytime. Every text box in your app automatically gets `Tab` inline completion and voice dictation support out of the box.

## Safe host capabilities

Open **App settings → Capabilities** in the preview bar to toggle what your app is allowed to use (checked means permanently allowed; unchecking revokes immediately with no nagging pop-ups):

- **Local data storage**: App data saves automatically to MTNode's local data directory — never inside the app folder — so updates never wipe your saved state.
- **Canvas, AI & Asset library**: Grant access when you want your app to trigger a workflow, generate text or images, or pull items from your Asset library.
- **Standalone & pop-out windows**: Launch apps from the Library or pop them out from the canvas into standalone desktop windows where `F1`–`F4` and `Ctrl + Tab` hotkeys work seamlessly.
- **Keep preview state**: Enabled by default in App settings so preview refreshes keep your place while iterating.

## Quick tips

- **Seeing the starter welcome page?** Just send your first request in the Develop chat — AI will replace the welcome page with your real app.
- **Ready to share?** Click **Publish** on the preview bar — see [Publish & manage app versions](#app-publish).
