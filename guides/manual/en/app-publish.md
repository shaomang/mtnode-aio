# Publish & manage app versions

Once your app looks good and launches cleanly, publish it to the cloud so other MTNode users can install it, try different versions, or fork it with their own ideas.

## Publishing a new app or update

1. Sign in to your MTNode account.
2. Go to **Apps → Develop**, pick your app, and click **Publish** on the preview bar.
   - Both first-time uploads and updates use the same **Publish** button: the first upload creates the listing, and later uploads append a new version (auto-incrementing the patch version, editable if you prefer).
3. **Review AI-drafted metadata**: Opening Publish drafts a title, description, tags, and version number from your app — always review and edit before submitting.
4. **Add screenshots**: Click **Capture app window** to launch and snap your app automatically, or pick local images (up to 8, drag to reorder, first image becomes the cover). When publishing an update, previously uploaded screenshots carry over automatically without re-uploading unchanged files.
5. **Confirm the declaration & upload**: Check the legal/originality declaration and submit.

> **App ID**: Locked by default so future updates attach to the same listing. Only click **Change ID** if you intentionally want to publish as a separate brand-new app.

## Versions & community forks

- **Version tree**: Every published version is organized under one listing, marking **Latest** and **Installed**. Users can install any version without losing their saved app data.
- **Automatic fork attribution**: If you copy someone else's app to Develop, improve it, and publish, attribution is carried over automatically and appears as a switchable branch alongside the original — branches stay isolated and never overwrite each other.

## Quotas & managing published apps

- **Default limits**: Up to **5 apps** per account (extra versions of an existing app don't count toward the 5-app cap) and **50 MB** total cloud storage across versions and screenshots. Current usage is always shown in the Publish footer; delete old versions right inside the dialog whenever you want to free space.
- **Edit or delete anytime**: Under **Apps in development → Published by me**, you can:
  - **Edit listing**: Update title, description, tags, icon, and screenshots immediately without issuing a new version number;
  - **Delete permanently**: Confirm by typing the app title to remove your branch, packages, and screenshots from the cloud (cannot be undone; forks by other authors and your local copy stay untouched).

## Upload troubleshooting

1. **Not signed in**: Sign in from the top right first.
2. **Declaration unchecked**: Check the compliance box before submitting.
3. **Quota reached**: Delete an older version in the Publish dialog to free space.
4. **App won't launch**: Return to Develop and click **Launch** to verify the entry page loads cleanly first.
