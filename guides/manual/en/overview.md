# Meet MTNode

MTNode AI Orchestrator is a local-first AI workspace on your desktop. Think of it as a visual workbench where ideas turn into repeatable workflows: drag a few nodes, connect them, click Run, and produce text, images, voiceovers, music, videos, research notes, or even your own standalone desktop apps — without writing code or sending private files to unknown platforms.

![MTNode workspace overview](img/mtnode-overview-ui.svg)

## Four main views

Switch between views anytime from the top-left bar:

- **Canvas**: Your core workspace. Lay out "Input → AI Processing → Save" as a visual flow. Change the input and re-run the whole chain in one click.
- **Session**: A full-screen AI agent chat. The agent can read files, write documents, search the web, drive a browser, and edit your canvas.
- **Expert team**: Build a roster of AI specialists (such as a product manager, editor, or financial advisor) tied to your current canvas. Chat 1-on-1 or hold a roundtable discussion to produce structured advice.
- **Research**: Ideal for papers, deep dives, and reading piles. Drop in PDFs and notes to build structured readings, term pages, a live knowledge graph, and slide decks.

On the right edge of the canvas you can also open the **Global assistant (✦)** anytime and ask it to build a complete workflow in one sentence.

## Your first workflow in 3 minutes

1. **Connect a model**: Open **Settings → Model services** and paste an API key (DeepSeek is a great default), or sign in to use MTNode Relay. See [Models & API keys](#providers).
2. **Ask the assistant to build a flow**: On the Canvas, click **✦** on the right and describe what you want — for example: "Build a flow that takes a product brief, rewrites it into a 300-word video script, and saves it."
3. **Swap in your material and run**: Replace the sample input with your own text and click **▶** to run the chain.

## Where your data lives

MTNode is local-first. **Every canvas, chat, asset, and setting stays on your computer** inside your app data folder (which you can move under **Settings → Data folder**).

> App updates replace the program folder, so MTNode never stores your files next to the executable. If a red warning appears in the top bar, click it to move your data folder cleanly.

## Themes & updates

- **Four themes**: Switch appearance under **Settings → Appearance**, including the clean **Daylight** light theme.
- **Instant bilingual UI**: Click the globe button at the top right to switch between Chinese and English — the manual and AI replies follow automatically.
- **Quiet updates**: New versions download and stage in the background without interrupting your work. When ready, click **Restart & update** whenever it suits you.
