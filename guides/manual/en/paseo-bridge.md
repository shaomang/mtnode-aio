# Phone handoff (Paseo)

Stepping away from your desk while an AI session is running? Install the **Paseo Phone Handoff** plugin to check progress, send follow-ups, steer mid-turn, or approve prompts straight from your phone.

## 1. One-time setup on your computer

1. Open **Plugins** in the top bar, find **Paseo Phone Handoff**, and click **Install** (everything installs through the guided install window — no manual terminal steps).
2. Click **Console** on the plugin card and complete two quick steps:
   - **Register MTNode sessions** with Paseo;
   - **Turn on remote access** (or simply click **Enable** on the plugin card to start the service and enable remote access together).
3. Install the **Paseo** app on your phone and scan the pairing QR code shown in the console.

> On the same local network (or Tailscale), you can also connect directly via your computer's IP address in the phone app.

## 2. What you can do from your phone

- **Check live progress**: See what your sessions are doing and read replies on the go.
- **Send or steer**: Queue a new prompt or nudge a running turn without waiting to get back to your desk.
- **Answer approval & question cards**: Approve tool permissions or answer multiple-choice prompts right from your phone so your computer keeps moving.
- **Stop anytime**: Cancel a runaway turn with one tap.

## 3. Scope & peace of mind

- **Sessions only**: Paseo never touches your canvas layout or app settings, and nothing runs until you explicitly click **Enable**.
- **Stops with MTNode**: The built-in background service shuts down cleanly whenever you close MTNode, or anytime you click **Disable**.

## Troubleshooting

- **Phone can't reach computer**: Make sure your computer is awake, MTNode is open, and the Paseo card shows **Enabled**.
- **Reinstall or uninstall**: Click **Reinstall** on the plugin card to open the install window, where **Uninstall** is also available at the bottom.
