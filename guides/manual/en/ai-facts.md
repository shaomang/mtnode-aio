# AI fact library

In long-running projects, repeating the same standing preferences in every new chat gets tiring fast. The **AI fact library** works like a compact sticky-note board beside the AI — one short rule per entry — so agents remember key conventions automatically.

## How it complements other knowledge tools

| Tool | Where to find it | Best for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **AI fact library** | Sidebar "AI fact library" | Short one-line rules and conventions read automatically by AI |
| **Team fact library** | Bottom of Expert team sidebar | Full reference documents read and edited by you and experts |
| **Data tables / DB** | Canvas Data table nodes | Large structured records (catalogs, logs, inventories) |

The AI fact library has two scopes: **Global facts** (personal preferences shared across every project) and **Project facts** (scoped to the current canvas or workspace).

## Keeping it sharp

- **Auto-captured**: As you build architectures or workflows with AI, confirmed rules are distilled into concise entries automatically.
- **Easy to edit**: Open the panel anytime to add, edit, pin critical rules, or remove outdated ones.
- **Stays lean**: Entries rank by usefulness and recency so stale notes fade out naturally instead of bloating every prompt. Pin anything you never want pruned.
